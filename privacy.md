**Version 1.2**
**Effective 2026-05-22**

## Summary

C-17 Courseware does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All course content, test attempts, profile information, and backups are stored only on the user's device. The app is currently distributed to stakeholders through Apple's **TestFlight** beta program; future distribution is planned through unit-managed MDM (e.g., INTUNE). Neither channel changes the policy below — no data leaves the device unless you explicitly export it yourself.

## What we do not do

- We do not collect personal information from you.
- We do not track you across apps, websites, or devices.
- We do not use analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting SDKs.
- We do not display advertising.
- We do not require an account or sign-in.
- We do not run our own servers; the app makes no network calls back to the developer.
- We do not include third-party trackers, advertising identifiers, or behavioral profiling.

## What stays on your device

The following data is stored locally on your iPad or iPhone and is never transmitted to the developer or to any third party:

- Your profile name and middle initial (manually entered by you).
- Imported course packages (`.zip` files you bring to the app).
- Lesson, module, and test attempt history.
- Practice mode statistics, flags, and per-question notes.
- Encrypted local backups of the above (AES-GCM authenticated encryption).
- App preferences (theme, alternate app icon, debug flags on Beta builds only).

You can export any of this data yourself via the in-app **Manage Data** screen, AirDrop a backup to another device, or wipe everything via **Clean Slate**. The app does not transmit any of this on your behalf.

## What you choose to share

The in-app **Submit Feedback** button (and the **Submit Testing Log** button on TestFlight builds) opens your default mail app with a pre-filled message draft. We see only what you choose to send to the support email address; we do not capture or relay anything in the background. The separate **Export App Logs** button lets you share the app's activity log to any app that accepts files (Mail, Outlook, Gmail, Files, etc.); when used with a mail app, the resulting draft is yours to review or redact before sending.

On TestFlight builds, Apple's own **TestFlight feedback** mechanism (the prompt that appears after you take a screenshot inside the app) is delivered directly to the developer via Apple. That submission is governed by Apple's standard TestFlight terms; the developer receives only the screenshot and any optional comment you add. See <https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/testflight.html> for Apple's full TestFlight terms.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

## Changes

If a future version of the app adds any form of data collection, analytics, crash reporting, or network call, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be reflected on the App Store product page.
